Inter-PBX trunks

SIP trunks to sibling PBXs, carried over the Tailscale transport.

Credentials are generated for you. Only Tailscale addresses (100.64.0.0/10) are accepted — SIP passwords should never cross an unencrypted network. After creating, you'll get the matching half to paste on the other PBX.
